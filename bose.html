Professional UX work · Bose · 2025–2026

Designing where ideas meet production.

My work at Bose brought together product thinking, visual design, and technical execution. As part of a four-person team, I collaborated across UX, marketing, and engineering to turn campaign and commerce requirements into responsive experiences customers could actually use.

RoleUX / Web Designer
EnvironmentCross-functional team
PlatformsFigma · Contentstack · Jira · AEM
ScopeSix global sites

The role

Designing within a real product ecosystem.

At Bose, design work didn't end at a Figma handoff. Every experience had to fit existing components, support customers across devices, and hold up through implementation and QA.

My role bridged early design reviews and production: assessing layouts against system constraints, refining copy and image placement, building within established CMS patterns, and partnering with teammates to resolve issues before release.

01 / Design collaboration

Refine the experience

Review layouts with UX and campaign partners; identify practical changes to hierarchy, imagery, and content placement.

02 / Systems thinking

Design within constraints

Work with approved components and patterns so distinctive campaigns still feel coherent across the wider shopping experience.

03 / Responsive QA

Protect the details

Check content hierarchy, image crops, accessibility considerations, and design fidelity across desktop and mobile.

04 / Cross-functional delivery

Help teams ship

Coordinate in Jira with marketing, UX, and engineering through review, implementation, troubleshooting, and launch preparation.
